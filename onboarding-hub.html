What's inside

Everything your onboarding needs, none of the busywork.

Personalized, not generic

Every new hire sees a journey shaped by their role, team and location — not one script for the whole company. An assistant trained only on your own content answers the questions that used to mean an email to HR, instantly and around the clock.

Progress people can see

A visible journey tracker turns onboarding into momentum instead of a folder of documents. Short pulse check-ins at day 7, 30 and 90 surface how things are really going, long before an exit interview would.

Built and owned by your team

Add a card, retire a stage, rewrite a section — your People team edits the hub directly, with nothing waiting on a developer or an agency. Manager and buddy nudges fire automatically, so tasks land on the right desk without anyone chasing.

Wired into what you already run

New-hire details flow straight from your HRIS, so nobody re-types a name or start date. Equipment and account provisioning trigger from onboarding milestones, and single sign-on picks up the moment a new hire's account exists.

Proof it's working

Completion rates, drop-off points and time-to-productivity, broken down by role, team or hiring manager — so you know what's landing and what to fix, instead of guessing from anecdotes.
