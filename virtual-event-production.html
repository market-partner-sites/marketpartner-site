● Market Partner LIVE

The most powerful platform for broadcasting to a global audience

Every event runs on a viewing platform built to your brand, not a generic player — with the interactivity to keep a remote audience engaged for the full session, and the production project-managed behind the scenes with our partner, SE1 Studios.

  • Highly flexible viewing platform, designed to your requirements
  • Totally on-brand with your company colours and logo
  • Breakout room functionality for smaller sessions
  • Live chat, networking, questions and messaging
  • Polling, voting, word clouds and live quizzes built in
  • Feedback forms, resources and gamification
  • On-demand viewing once the live session ends
  • Real-time analytics on attendance and engagement
  • Managed and monitored by our in-house platform team throughout the event
  • Combine the virtual experience with on-site attendance for a complete attendee experience
